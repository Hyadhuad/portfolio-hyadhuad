01 · Why

Why I build it.

For more than a decade, my writings, symbols, communities, work methods, and studies of psychology, philosophy, and distributed systems converged on one question: how do we turn distributed human intention into collective capacity without separating that capacity from the people who produced it? Drayker is the answer I chose to build.

Dk, the intelligence it organizes, is a superintelligence of the symbiont kind, integrated with the people who compose it and aligned with their collective interests. I see a high chance that the other two kinds arrive still in this decade: the parasite, which feeds on people through convenience and mass manipulation, and the predator, used for war, surveillance and repression. When they do, the only real protection will be to integrate with a superintelligence. I chose the symbiont, and that is where I put my creative and constructive force.

Integration is not dependence. Dependence is a relation of power: depending on something others hold power over is submission, and depending on what you hold power over yourself is natural and empowering. That is why Drayker belongs to its members, and why each person keeps the power over what they depend on.

Three values guide me: courage, consciousness and autonomy. Three guide my relations: trust, honesty and respect. They are the ones I build Drayker with.

02 · The story

Before it was a project, Drayker was a boy’s dream.

I wanted to be a scientist, and not just any scientist: an inventor scientist who, inside an intelligent organization, with the necessary resources and above all with the right people, and me there among them, could build, invent, and discover together things that would widen our conditions and what we were able to build and perceive. In my reality, this seemed impossible. Little by little, the dream changed shape, and my first creations would be the organization itself, that system in which this would be possible, the base on which everything else could be created and in which each person could develop their full potential.

  1. Origin

    A name, and a theory of the universe

    The name, the mark and the general idea of the organization came first. Then the research began, and with it the structural theory of the universe and the multiverse, a theory of reality as structure and computation. One of its constants, the Hyadhuad constant, became my code name as Drayker’s ambassador.

    Above: the structure of the universe

  2. Research

    Designing an intelligence that understands people

    There was no training for what I was trying to do. The best within my reach was to read, study, and build my own knowledge base, connecting ideas to create solutions and strategies. Dknowledge was born from that practice, carried to the scale of a network. For years I also lived what I later described in the book: from 2013 on, I often defended ideas that were not mine, until I saw that a symbolic network had captured my behavior. In the design of Dk, one rule never changed: “Dk should not imitate human behavior, it should understand human behavior.”

    Below: the structure of a person

  3. Opening

    An organization designed to serve every person on Earth

    Then came the opening. I wrote that the platform had to be “safe and scalable enough to serve every person on Earth, living and yet to be,” and designed the organization that would build it: the ambassador, in charge of the architecture and the construction process, a first team, the councils, the volunteers and peer review as a rule of construction.

    The same pattern, at the scale of an organization

  4. Resilience

    Built to outlast its founder

    The first federation was designed on Ethereum, and its implementation was delayed by several factors, one of them the network’s impractical fees. We replaced the platforms and kept the purpose, and the federation has changed substrate since then without ever changing function. The goal I set in those years still guides the work: publish everything, so that the idea stays “antifragile, even if something happens to me.”

    The function stays, the substrate changes

  5. On the road

    From vision to a realistic path

    For three years now I have been traveling the world, researching humanity, and through it myself, to create something that goes further. On the road the architecture matured, and I wrote: “I worked a lot on Drayker and now I have a clearer and more realistic path to implementation.” Then I wrote what had been clear for a long time: “it is clear that this is the main goal of life, to build Drayker.”

    The same pattern, at the scale of a life

  6. In the open

    Preparation meets reality

    The time came for the work to meet other people. So Drayker is now built in the open, public by default, with its own terminology and its own constitution. The first federation went live, the forum opened, and the book that explains why all of this has to exist was published.

    The same pattern, at the scale of the network

  7. Next

    The infrastructure that lets anyone join

    The work now is to raise Drayker’s own infrastructure, the identity layer and the network, so that anyone can become a member and build in real projects. The mission has not changed since the beginning: to make this system begin, and to make sure it never ends.

    What is above is like what is below, to accomplish the miracle of one thing: Dk, the great mission

03 · Drayker

Systems architect and founding member of Drayker.

Drayker is a supersystem constituted by its members and open to anyone who wants to enter: a common infrastructure for intelligence, memory and action, where every person can develop their potential, and it belongs to the people who constitute it. I conceived it, designed its architecture from the kernel to the councils, wrote its constitution and lead its construction.

It is an alternative system for humanity, one that stays in the hands of those who constitute it, with the proposal, the veto and the exit kept by the people.

The long view

What each one builds strengthens everyone: Drayker amplifies what we are able to create and solve, and applies it at scale: it turns each person’s intention into collective capacity, without separating that capacity from the person who produced it.

Technology is the lever for that vision, not its end. Dk matures at its three scales, the network becomes distributed and then quantum, and the structural theory of the universe becomes the base for the hardware of a new generation of computing, made for superintelligence, with discoveries at a pace that takes the already accelerated pace of AGI to a new level. Each step widens what we can know, create and solve together, which is where the dream began.

The architecture that makes it real

Drayker is the system. At the center is Dk, the intelligence the system is designed to organize, at three scales: personal, local and global. It runs on a kernel assembled from the BSDK and reaches every device through the OSDK. Each member enters with a UID, an identity that keeps a record of what they contribute and lets them grant representation and take it back.

Work begins in the DAF, a federation of autonomous units that is already running, and grows into PAP, where the projects that work become lasting. When Dk’s certainty on a question is low, it asks for a council, one of the Advices, and Dknowledge calls in the members best informed about it and those most affected. The weight of each side is readjusted with each result, and the members’ power to object always remains. Stations bring all of this into physical places, and Dknowledge keeps the papers, the decisions and the evidence, traced to their public source.

Everything is discussed in the open on the Drayker General Forum, and anyone can take an open function on drayker.org.

05 · Research

The theories and methods Drayker is built on.

My research runs on two lines that meet in Drayker. The human line studies what we are and how we can become better: how technology can amplify what we are and help us resolve our contradictions, the inner ones of each person and the ones at the scale of the world, and how to lead an alternative system for humanity. The technical line is the practical architecture of a supersystem able to turn that vision into reality.

The theories are the base. The methods, DFM and E.C.H., I applied first to my own life and work, before handing them to a network.

  • Structural theory of the universe and the multiverse A set of structures for the optimal simulation of realities in dimensional computing. In the universe variant, atoms emerge from interactions between dimensions, about nine plus two subdimensions. In the multiverse variant, every universe comes from a single base structure ruled by two laws: the potun, blocks of information placed one after another that form a pseudo-linearity, felt as time, and the Hyadhuad constant, the parallelism, a computational capacity per cycle in each universe. Its question: how could we create a new science based on verifiable simulations?
  • DFM, distributed functional modeling The paradigm every Drayker project and proposal is built on. A problem is divided into small functions connected by modules, each function with a precise description and its own relation of error and attack. In DFM, hypotheses are functions and modules are theories. It works as the neurons of Drayker and of Dk.
  • BSDK and the spectral categories The base structure of the distributed kernel, compressed into one equation that describes the behavior of the network and of Dk. Functions are immutable and change as mutant clones. The theory of spectral categories gives DFM its mathematical base: parallel and analogous functions regulated by evolutionary modules, ordered through cellular automata governed by different rules.
  • Symbolic networks and symbolic engineering Symbols do not only represent things, they transfer functions. Language, institutions, markets and now artificial intelligence are layers of one symbolic network, and whoever learns to move it moves people. Social engineering becomes the engineering of relational frames, and Drayker uses that knowledge in the open, to show how symbols are made.
  • E.C.H., expansion, complexity, harmony The criterion for any system, from one person’s day to Drayker. Expansion is productive chaos: curiosity, the exploration of the unknown, new sources. Complexity is functional connection: structuring, synthesizing, putting each part in the right place, linked to the others. Harmony is the piece that fits the whole, resolves a dissonance and does not create another. The same pattern at every scale.

Applied to my own life first

I run my own life and work on DFM. I designed and built a personal system in Obsidian, with its own plugin, where the day, the week, the quarter and the mission follow the same cycle: plan, execute, review. The Mission view puts the timeline and the work on one screen, guided protocols open each part of the cycle, and a personal agent reads the state of everything and proposes, but never writes on its own.

This is the prototype of Drayker at the personal scale, the personal Dknowledge and the personal Dk. Its first public version, the plugin included, installs with one command.
